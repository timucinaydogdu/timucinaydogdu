## About

I am building a career in **data center systems administration and infrastructure auditing**. My path started in interior design and CAD drafting (six years, AutoCAD and SolidWorks certified) and is now moving into IT infrastructure, supported by degrees in computer programming and business administration.

I learn by building: home labs, written runbooks, and documented evidence for every exercise. My long-term aim is to install, manage and monitor data center servers, then audit operations against international standards such as **ISO/IEC 27001**, **ANSI/TIA-942** and the **Uptime Institute Tier** framework.
